FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How fast do you respond to support requests?

Our average response time is 15 minutes during business hours, and the Business and Enterprise plans include 24/7 coverage. Around 97% of issues are fixed remotely on first contact, so most problems are solved before an on-site visit is even needed.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. All our plans are monthly with no lock-in. You can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. We also offer one-off projects and annual maintenance contracts (AMC) if you prefer fixed annual pricing.

Can you support our office if you're not located nearby?

Yes. Most support happens remotely — our helpdesk can securely access your systems from anywhere. For hands-on work, our engineers cover Hyderabad and Secunderabad, and our remote helpdesk supports teams across India.

We already have an internal IT person. Can you work alongside them?

Absolutely. Many clients use us as an extension of their internal team — handling overflow tickets, projects, security and after-hours coverage while their in-house staff focuses on day-to-day operations.

What's included in the free IT audit?

We review your devices, network, software licences, backups and security posture, then give you a plain-English report with prioritised recommendations. It's completely free and there's no obligation to buy anything.

Do you supply hardware and software licences too?

Yes. We source laptops, desktops, networking gear, CCTV and printers at competitive prices, and we handle Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and antivirus licensing — so you have one point of contact for everything.
